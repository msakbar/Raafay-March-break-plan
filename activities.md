### Museums & Exhibits

### Royal Ontario Museum — BEES: A Story of Survival
- **Link:** https://www.rom.on.ca/whats-on/exhibitions/bees-story-survival
- **Location:** 100 Queens Park, Toronto
- **Type:** Indoor
- **Cost:** Adults $23, Children (4-14) $14.50, **Children under 3 FREE**. Exhibition surcharge may apply (~$6 extra).
- **Duration:** 2-4 hours
- **Hours:** Daily 10:00 am – 5:30 pm
- **Age suitability:** All ages
- **Special exhibit:** BEES: A Story of Survival (May 16 – Oct 18, 2026). Created with National Museums Liverpool & artist Wolfgang Buttress. Immersive environments, art installations, soundscapes exploring 120 million years of bee adaptation.
- **Family highlights:**
  - Hands-on pollinator activity: step into the role of a pollinator using touchable flower and hive models
  - Larger-than-life bee images at stations highlighting unique adaptations
  - Interactive cart exploring how native bees nest in logs, stems, leaves, and underground
- **Events on May 31:**
  - Museum Highlights Tour: 11:00–11:45 am (free with admission)
  - Art & Culture Tour: 12:00–12:45 pm (free with admission)
  - "Let it Bee" Gardening Workshop: 1:00–4:00 pm (adults only)
- **Also on display:** Shokkan: Japanese Art Through Touch (Apr 4 – Sep 7, 2026) — 100+ objects with hands-on experiences
- **Notes:** 2-year-old enters free. 4-year-old pays child admission. The BEES exhibit is very tactile and interactive — great for young kids.
- **Status:** 💡

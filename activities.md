## Active Play

### Joyride 150 Indoor Bike Park
- **Link:** https://www.joyride150.com/
- **Location:** 150 Bullock Dr, Markham
- **Type:** Indoor
- **Cost:** Free entry for under 4 / Rental bikes $14.99 (6 & under) / $5.31 registration fee for new riders
- **Duration:** Half day+
- **Hours:** Drop-in, Mon–Thu 3–10pm, Fri–Sun 10am–10pm (March break hours may differ — check site)
- **Age suitability:** 2+ (free balance bikes for little ones)
- **Notes:** Going with friend Zain. Bring socks. Helmets required (available to rent). Pump tracks, foam pit, beginner sections.
- **Status:** ✅ Monday March 16

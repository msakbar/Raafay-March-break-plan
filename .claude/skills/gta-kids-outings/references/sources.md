## Venue anchors by category (re-verify hours for the date)

**Indoor active**: Joyride 150 (Markham, bike park, open late), indoor playgrounds (call first — birthday buyouts), Fantasy Fair (Woodbine Mall), Kids Fun City.
**Animals**: Ripley's Aquarium (open late), Toronto Zoo, High Park Zoo (free), Riverdale Farm (free), Reptilia, African Lion Safari.
**Museums**: ROM, AGO, Bata Shoe Museum, Little Canada, Museum of Illusions, Aga Khan Museum, Toronto Railway Museum, KidSpark (Ontario Science Centre at Harbourfront — main OSC building is closed).
**Shows (ages 2–6)**: Young People's Theatre, TYT Theatre, Meridian Hall family shows, WeeFestival, Living Arts Centre Mississauga.
**Free / low-key**: Toronto Public Library storytimes, EarlyON drop-ins (works for both kids), splash pads (seasonal), Harbourfront Centre, Biidaasige Park.
**Seasonal**:
- Spring: maple syrup festivals (Terra Cotta, Kortright), Sugar Shack TO, farms reopening (Chudleigh's, Springridge, Pingle's, Brooks).
- Summer: CNE (late Aug–Labour Day), Centreville, splash pads, strawberry picking (mid-June+).
- Fall: PumpkinFest Downsview, Pumpkins After Dark (Markham/Milton, evenings), RBG Great Pumpkin Trail (Burlington, mid/late Oct), Wonderland daytime kids' Halloween event (not Haunt), Woodbridge Fair (Thanksgiving weekend), farm pumpkin patches.
- Winter: holiday light walks, Distillery Winter Village, skating rinks, Lumiere at Ontario Place.
**Eid / community**: ISNA Canada EidFest, local masjid family events.
